《廣孝歸宗論》是華文基督教單張，館目歸於韋廉臣（Alexander Williamson），估刊於 1870 年代福州。文章引用孔子論孝，從事親、事君與敬上出發，主張敬奉上帝是孝的根本。文中以衣食與物產說明上帝之恩，又以亂臣逆子為喻，論辯背離上帝與拜神佛求赦。中研院近史所一篇研究引錄同作者 1858 年〈論孝〉的相合開頭。此本轉錄提供單張全篇及原圖欄位定位，可用於比對報刊與傳教印件中的孝道論述。

本資料集以哈佛燕京藏本 DRS 431236545 為底本，共取 1 幅固定影像，其中 1 幅用於轉錄正文，提供 JSON、TXT、TEI 和逐項覆核紀錄。此版為 AI 校讀稿，38 項比對中保留 23 項未解。人工全文二校待完成。

Guang xiao gui zong lun is a Chinese Christian broadside attributed in the catalog to Alexander Williamson and dated there to the 1870s in Fuzhou. It quotes Confucius on filial piety and draws on duties to parents and rulers to argue that honoring God is the foundation of filial conduct. It illustrates divine beneficence through provisions such as food and clothing, then argues against turning to other deities or the Buddha for pardon. A study published by Academia Sinica quotes a matching opening passage from Williamson’s 1858 essay Lun xiao. This dataset transcribes the full broadside and records column locations for comparison across nineteenth-century print genres.

This dataset uses 1 fixed image from Harvard-Yenching Library DRS 431236545. The main text is transcribed from 1 page. The AI-reviewed JSON, TXT and TEI transcription retains 23 unresolved comparison items. Full human collation is outstanding.

## 資料與校讀

- data/document.json 保留歷史文字、原圖位置、疑讀與缺口。
- data/text.txt 與 data/text.tei.xml 為全文與 TEI，附文字單位的對應檔。
- data/review.json 保留 38 項比對、23 項未解及採用紀錄。
- data/manifest.json 與 PACKAGE-SHA256SUMS.txt 記錄固定發行的檔案雜湊。
